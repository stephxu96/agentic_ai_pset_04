This release introduces smarter configuration tools, improved design analysis, and new mobile
capabilities to streamline your on-shape workflows.
Configuring complex designs just got faster, you can now automatically propagate configuration
inputs to reference part studios, assemblies, and variable studios.
On-shape matches, compatible inputs, and values eliminating repetitive configuration setup
and making it much easier to manage complex parametrically-driven designs.
Persistent center of mass is now available in assemblies, display it for individual components,
or the entire assembly.
We locations updating automatically as components move.
You can even display the dimension to the center of mass and drawings, helping you accurately
evaluate and document weight distribution.
Our studios can now display important properties directly in the parts list, including
part numbers, revisions, and states.
This provides an immediate overview of your components without opening individual properties,
making it easier to manage and verify design information.
Comments can now be attached directly to drawing views and annotations, selecting
a tagging comment takes you straight to the reference location, making design feedback
easier to understand and reducing confusion during drawing reviews.
A new dedicated four-short and dimension command makes diameter-dimensioning easier, quickly
create four-short and diameter dimensions using a centerline and selected geometry, or switch
between four-short and standard dimensions as needed.
This provides greater flexibility for clearly documenting your designs.
A synchronous simulation now provides more control over your analysis, run simulations
from a version's default position, cancel unnecessary jobs, and delete completed results.
Unnotifications also keep you informed as simulations progress, allowing you to focus
on design work instead of constantly checking simulation statuses.
Reflection analysis now integrates with unshaped shared image library, choose from standard
custom reflection images, and instantly preview how they interact with your surfaces.
This makes evaluating surface smoothness and continuity easier while keeping all of your
visual reference assets in one place.
Maintaining feature script dependencies just got easier.
A new search option helps you find standard library imports by version or version range across
your workspace.
You can even update matching imports simultaneously, dramatically reducing the upper
required to maintain and modernize custom features.
API keys now support configurable expiration dates, set a time to live when creating a key
and receive email confirmation of its expiration settings.
This provides better visibility and control over API credentials, helping organizations
manage access more effectively.
Managing cam jobs is now simpler, select any job element and press delete to remove it, eliminating
unnecessary context menu interactions and making every day job cleanup faster.
Cam Studio now lets you override machine retract settings for individual operations.
Keep your global defaults while customizing or disabling retract behavior wherever necessary.
Providing greater control over toolpath behavior without affecting the rest of your
machining job.
Moment and reality viewing is now available on supported Android devices, place your
on-shaped design into real-world environments, adjust their scale and orientation, and explore
the latest model geometry directly on your device.
This makes it easier to visualize and communicate designs outside the CAD workspace.
Editing in context on Android now provides clearer labels and improved visual feedback, making
it easier to identify assembly references and understand how parts relate to surrounding
geometry.
The Android comments panel has moved to the left side of the screen, matching the familiar
on-shaped interface.
You can now create, resolve, and filter comments from one consistent location, making
collaboration more intuitive.
Android users can now attach comments directly to sketch dimensions, take a dimension to
provide specific feedback, then return directly to the reference geometry from the comet,
making design discussions more precise and actionable.
The customizable S-key toolbar is now accessible using a long press with the Apple Pencil
or your finger on iPad, quickly access your favorite modeling tools without interrupting
your workflow.
Apple Pencil Double Tap also provides convenient access to context menus, making touch-space
CAD more efficient.
AI Advisor is now available in on-shaped for iOS, get answers to on-shaped questions directly
from your mobile device, helping you discover workflows and solve problems without leaving
your design environment.
Education enterprise users can now make personal copies of their documents when permitted
by administrators.
Students can preserve their assignments and projects in their personal on-shaped accounts,
allowing them to retain access to their work even after leaving their educational
organization.
To learn more about these updates, visit on-shaped and see more in-depth videos for each
new feature.
