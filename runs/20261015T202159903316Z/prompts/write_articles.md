# Write articles

You are writing for *Agentic 3D*. Turn the supplied brief into one article for technical product managers and engineering leaders who understand LLMs and basic 3D and CAD concepts but do not follow benchmarks or demos daily. Follow the editorial policy's voice: direct, precise, skeptical without being cynical.

## Input

One brief (argument, synthesis, limitations, typed claims with evidence) and the evidence behind it (text and timestamped transcript segments, with source version details). The as-of date comes from the run.

## Requirements

- **Length:** about 600-900 words, excluding references.
- **Structure:** open with the question and the answer. Develop the argument. Include the synthesis, where evidence from several sources is connected, and explain what the analysis adds beyond any one source. Address the limitation or counterargument in the brief. End with what the reader should do or watch next.
- **Distinguish four things, in the text:** reported facts, source claims (say who claims it), your interpretation, and remaining uncertainty. Do not state a source claim as fact.
- **Apply the core test:** for each key capability claim, say what check observed it and whether that check matches the claim.
- **Startups:** cover the startup or startups in the brief as the brief's role specifies. Say when the claimant also built the benchmark or the grader is not public.
- **Claim markers:** keep each claim's marker (such as `01-c1`) inline where the claim appears, so the draft stays inspectable. Use only claims from the brief. Do not add facts, numbers, or quotes that are not in the evidence.
- **References:** end with a reference list that resolves each marker to a reader-facing citation: title, publisher, publication date, URL, and, for audio or video, the timestamp of the passage. Internal IDs alone are not citations.
- **Quotes:** paraphrase. Any quotation is short and exact, and appears at most once per source.
- **As-of line:** state the as-of date near the top.
- **Disclosure:** note that the article was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by the author.
- **Video link:** include the line `Watch the video version: [VIDEO LINK]` for the author to fill after publication.

## Style

Plain language. Define jargon in a clause. No hype words, no investment advice, no calls to buy. State what the evidence supports and no more. Keep facts consistent with the companion video script, which is generated from the same brief.

Output the article as Markdown only.
